# Assignment 1 Note

`customer_id` is generated for every order, so it links `orders` to `customers` one-to-one, while `customer_unique_id` identifies the real person across orders. I would count customers with `customer_unique_id`, because `customer_id` overcounts repeat buyers (99,441 IDs but only 96,096 unique people). `order_items` is the junction table: it resolves the many-to-many between orders and products (an order holds many products, a product appears in many orders) and also links sellers to orders. The data is stricter than my diagram for customers → orders: I drew "one or many", but every `customer_id` has exactly one order (min 1, max 1). The circle on orders → order_items is justified by the data, because 775 orders (mostly unavailable and canceled) have no items.
